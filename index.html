The problem

The money runs out before the month does.

Student allowances and stipends often arrive as lump sums and get split across cash and e-wallets. Without a clear plan, it can be difficult to see where the money goes and make it last.

2 in 10Filipinos scored 100% on basic financial literacy questions, as cited in the Finuity campaign materials (BSP, via Medalla, 2022).

Meet a smarter money routine.

Finuity helps students become more aware of their spending through simple, intentional tracking.

  • See multiple wallets in one view.
  • Log income and expenses in one place.
  • Set budgets, goals, and spending limits.
  • Learn practical money habits with Fin.

About Finuity

Helping students make every peso count.

Finuity is a student-focused personal finance management concept developed to make money tracking more approachable and financial learning part of everyday life.
